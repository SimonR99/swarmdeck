| Millimetre solver refinements churned every robot's frame; three of four robots stalled | Measured on benchbot 2026-09-19 (mission 1a8cc114, inter-robot closures on, four robots exploring for 243 s): the peers accepted 100 inter-robot closures (2 intra-robot), the optimizer reported a new solution every 2 to 3 s, and each report moved some pose by a few millimetres, above the 5 mm / 0.05 degree tolerance under which the bridge adopted a result (a new `correction_revision`, a rewritten snapshot, a MOLA rebuild and re-index). Every consumer treats an adoption as a frame change (routes re-validated, plans refused as `requested snapshot is not current`, `a different snapshot failed indexed publication`, `MOLA map snapshot changed during planning`, `indexed mapping authority changed during query`), so 24 of the 36 plan rejections were this churn and three of four robots stalled with `controller recovery timed out waiting for a replacement path`. The whole run's 270 closures improved the inter-platform floor placement by no more than 5 cm, so a millimetre refinement is not worth a frame change. Rule (`autonomy/cslam.py`): a result is adopted only when it moves some pose by more than 5 cm or 0.5 degrees against the currently adopted poses (`SOLUTION_CHANGE_TRANSLATION_M`, `SOLUTION_CHANGE_ROTATION_RAD`); a move below 0.25 m and 2 degrees (`SOLUTION_LARGE_CHANGE_*`) is a refinement, adopted at most once per `SOLUTION_ADOPTION_MIN_INTERVAL_S` (10 s of the bridge's monotonic clock) after the last adoption; a larger move, a new anchor (a merge) or a keyframe the frame never placed is adopted at once. A held result only advances `solver_order` (ordering), like a result that moves nothing; its poses are not kept, and because every report is compared with the adopted poses the deferred refinements accumulate and land in full with the first report after the interval, or at once when they cross the large threshold. `status.json` splits accepted results into `solutions` (adopted), `solution_results_deferred` and `solution_results_unchanged`, and shows `last_solver_order` against `last_solution_order` with the pending `deferred_solution` (order, translation, rotation). Trap: a robot's adopted frame can lag the solver by up to the interval plus one solver report, so its replica publication and mapping authority carry an older order than its neighbours'; the composite and merged views already tolerate that (`compatible_solution_orders`: one optimizer, clocks apart), and a goal is dispatched with the robot's own order. |
